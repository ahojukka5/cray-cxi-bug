768 nodes / 6144 ranks, payload 0.076 MB/peer:
  22251807  FAILED at round 1 of 40  (elapsed 1.0 s)
  22251811  FAILED at round 1 of 40  (elapsed 1.0 s)

Both aborted on the first exchange. No Julia, no application code, no
compute kernels: MPI over HIP device buffers and nothing else.

This matters because the same scale had failed seven times out of seven
in the application (Lieb.jl jobs 22223239, 22229860, 22231048, 22233653,
22240542, 22240544, 22240545), every one on its first Hx, while 384 and
512 nodes produced clean nine-repeat runs in the same period. The bare
reproducer reaching the same verdict rules out the application as the
cause.

Whether 6144 ranks is a specific boundary or only a point on a rising
curve is not settled: the 512, 640 and 1024 node arms were still queued.
